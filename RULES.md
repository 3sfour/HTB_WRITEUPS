# Writeup Rules

## Do
- Write up **retired** Machines, Challenges, Sherlocks and similar content only.
- Explain *why* each step worked: the vulnerability, the root cause, the fix.
- Redact flags (`HTB{...}`, `user.txt`, `root.txt` contents) and any live credentials.
- Credit tools and other people's research you relied on.
- Use your own notes, commands and output. Never invent steps you did not perform.

## Don't
- Don't publish solutions for **active** Machines or Challenges.
- Don't publish flags, or anything that lets a reader skip the work.
- Don't publish Pro Lab or Cloud Lab solutions. Keep those as private notes.
- Don't copy HTB official writeups or paid walkthroughs.
- Don't include your VPN config, API tokens or account details.

## Redaction checklist
- [ ] No flags
- [ ] No VPN keys or tokens
- [ ] Target IPs are fine to keep (they are lab-internal), but remove anything personal
- [ ] Screenshots cropped to remove usernames, emails and browser tabs
- [ ] Box is confirmed retired
